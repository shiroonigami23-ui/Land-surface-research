## About this encyclopedia

**Land & Field Research** is a long-form, sourced reference to Earth's land, inspired by references such as FAO, the UNCCD, the USDA, NCERT geography and Wikipedia's geography portal.

- **Every country** (198) and **all seven continents**, each with a live photo, overview, climate, landscape and the crops it grows.
- **Every kind of land region**: mountain peaks and ranges, plateaus, hot, cold, coastal and polar deserts, glaciers and ice sheets, tundra and permafrost, rainforests, grasslands, wetlands, canyons, volcanoes and caves.
- **Land species**: mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, insects, trees, flowers and fungi, with photos, sounds and a full live article.
- **Places**: waterfalls, dams, caves, underground cities, national parks, tiger reserves and protected areas.
- **Soils**, **climate zones and rainfall**, **droughts and disasters**, **crops** and **fruits**.
- **A live tracker** of land research and **animated statistics**.
