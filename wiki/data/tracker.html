1 · Amazon deforestation (Brazil)
**Status:** Brazil's PRODES satellite system measured **3,335 km²** of clear-cutting in the Amazon between August 2025 and July 2026, a **41.8% fall** and the lowest since records began in 1988. The faster DETER alert system recorded about 2,874 km² (down ~36%). Clearing in the **Cerrado** savanna fell about 24% to ~5,517 km². **Why it matters:** the Amazon stores roughly 150–200 billion tonnes of carbon and recycles much of South America's rainfall. Parts of the southeastern Amazon already emit more carbon than they absorb. **Next:** the consolidated PRODES figure and whether the trend holds through the dry season and the 2026 Brazilian political cycle. Sources: [INPE TerraBrasilis](https://terrabrasilis.dpi.inpe.br/) · [Amazon rainforest catalog entry](/wiki/landforms/amazon-rainforest.html)
2 · India monsoon 2026
**Status:** June–September rainfall was **759.4 mm vs a normal of 868.6 mm (87%)**. East & Northeast India received 74% and the South Peninsula 76% of normal. **Next:** effects on the rabi (winter) crop, reservoir storage and groundwater; IMD's first 2027 forecast in April. Sources: [IMD](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/) · [Rainfall and the monsoon](/wiki/climate/rainfall-and-the-monsoon.html)
3 · Global glacier loss
**Status:** Glaciers lost **408 ± 132 Gt** in hydrological year 2025 (~1.1 mm of sea-level rise). Loss rose from under 100 Gt a year (1976–1995) to ~390 Gt a year (2016–2025); cumulative loss since 1975 is ~9,583 Gt (26.4 mm). 2025 was the 4th consecutive year with mass loss in all 19 glacier regions. Sources: [WGMS](https://wgms.ch/global-glacier-state/) · [Copernicus Climate Change Service](https://climate.copernicus.eu/) · [Snow, ice and permafrost](/wiki/earth/snow-ice-and-permafrost.html)
4 · Desertification (UNCCD COP17)
**Status:** COP17 of the UN Convention to Combat Desertification concluded in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, in August 2026. The UN says up to **40%** of land is degraded and droughts have become **29% more frequent and longer since 2000**. Sources: [UNCCD](https://www.unccd.int/) · [Land degradation](/wiki/soil/land-degradation-and-desertification.html)
5 · Drought cost
**Status:** Land degradation and drought cost about **$878 billion a year**; Sub-Saharan Africa holds 45% of the world's degraded land. Sources: [UNCCD](https://www.unccd.int/land-and-life/drought/overview) · [Droughts](/wiki/climate/droughts.html)
6 · Soil health
**Status:** FAO's *Status of the World's Soil Resources* found about a third of soils moderately to highly degraded. India's Soil Health Card scheme continues field testing. Sources: [FAO Global Soil Partnership](https://www.fao.org/global-soil-partnership/en/) · [What is soil?](/wiki/soil/what-is-soil.html)
