# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Albedo** | How much sunlight a surface reflects; snow is high, forest and soil low |
| **Alluvium** | Sediment deposited by rivers |
| **Aquifer** | Underground rock or sediment layer that holds and yields groundwater |
| **Biome** | Large region defined by climate and vegetation, e.g. rainforest, tundra |
| **Bhabar / terai** | Pebbly foothill belt and the marshy belt below it along the Himalaya |
| **Chernozem** | "Black earth", a very fertile grassland soil |
| **Desertification** | Land degradation in drylands, mainly caused by human activity and drought |
| **El Niño / La Niña** | Warm and cool phases of the tropical Pacific that shift rainfall worldwide |
| **Erosion** | Removal of soil or rock by water, wind or ice |
| **ITCZ** | Intertropical Convergence Zone, the rainy belt where trade winds meet |
| **Karst** | Landscape of caves and sinkholes formed by dissolving limestone |
| **Kharif / rabi / zaid** | India's monsoon, winter and summer crop seasons |
| **Köppen classification** | System that groups climates by temperature and rainfall |
| **Laterite** | Iron- and aluminium-rich leached soil of hot wet regions |
| **Loess** | Wind-blown silt that forms fertile soils |
| **Orographic rain** | Rain caused by air rising over mountains |
| **Permafrost** | Ground frozen for at least two years in a row |
| **Plateau** | Elevated, fairly flat tableland |
| **Rain shadow** | Dry area on the downwind side of mountains |
| **Regur** | India's black cotton soil |
| **Salinisation** | Build-up of salt in soil |
| **Snow line** | Altitude above which snow lasts all year |
| **Tundra** | Treeless cold plain with permafrost |
| **Watershed** | Area draining to one river or stream |
