Soil erosion

Soil erosion and agricultural sustainability

Conventional ploughing erodes soil 10–100× faster than it forms; no-till and conservation farming can close the gap.

Soil carbon sequestration impacts on global climate change and food security

Restoring degraded soils could store large amounts of carbon while raising crop yields.

Land use

Global consequences of land use

Croplands and pastures now rival forests as Earth's largest land cover, trading short-term needs against long-term ecosystem services.

Food security

Solutions for a cultivated planet

Halting farmland expansion, closing yield gaps, using water and fertiliser better, shifting diets and cutting waste could double food supply sustainably.

Forests

High-resolution global maps of 21st-century forest cover change

Landsat mapping showed 2.3 million km² of forest lost and 0.8 million km² gained in 2000–2012.

Climate zones

Present and future Köppen-Geiger climate classification maps at 1-km resolution

The standard modern 1-km Köppen map, with projected shifts of climate zones by 2071–2100.

Glaciers

Accelerated global glacier mass loss in the early twenty-first century

Glaciers lost ~267 Gt a year in 2000–2019, accelerating, contributing about a fifth of sea-level rise.

Glaciers

Community estimate of global glacier mass changes from 2000 to 2023

A reconciled estimate from all methods: glaciers lost about 5% of their ice since 2000, with loss rising by over a third in the second decade.

Groundwater

Satellite-based estimates of groundwater depletion in India

GRACE gravity data showed Rajasthan, Punjab and Haryana losing ~17.7 km³ of groundwater a year to irrigation.

Agriculture origins

Evolution, consequences and future of plant and animal domestication

Why farming arose in only a few homelands and how it spread and shaped human history.

Green Revolution

Green Revolution: Impacts, limits, and the path ahead

High-yield varieties averted famine and saved land but left problems of water, soil and nutrition to solve.

Soil erosion

An assessment of the global impact of 21st century land use change on soil erosion

Global soil erosion by water was modelled at ~36 billion tonnes a year, rising with cropland expansion.

Drought

Climate change in the Fertile Crescent and implications of the recent Syrian drought

The 2007–2010 Syrian drought was the worst on record and made 2–3× more likely by human-caused warming.

Soil mapping

SoilGrids250m: Global gridded soil information based on machine learning

Machine learning on ~150,000 soil profiles produced global 250 m maps of soil properties and classes.

Crops & climate

Climate trends and global crop production since 1980

Warming since 1980 cut global maize and wheat yields by several percent compared with a no-warming world.

Crops & climate

Temperature increase reduces global yields of major crops in four independent estimates

Each 1°C of warming cuts global yields of maize ~7.4%, wheat ~6.0%, rice ~3.2% and soybean ~3.1%.
