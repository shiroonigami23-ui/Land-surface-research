# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-15
### Added
- First release of the land-surface encyclopedia: 19 articles; 198 countries; 7 continents; 84 landforms; 378 land species with sound descriptions and auto-playing recordings; 68 waterfalls, dams, caves and underground sites; 48 protected areas; 27 soils; 16 climate zones; 38 crops; 37 fruits; 23 droughts and disasters; 16 research papers.
- Live tracker (Amazon deforestation, India monsoon 2026, glaciers, UNCCD COP17, drought cost, soil health).
- Animated statistics page, animated terrain hero, scroll-reveal and count-up animations.
- Nano Banana `prompts.md` for any entry without a photo.
- GitHub Actions for build validation and open-access PDF mirroring.
