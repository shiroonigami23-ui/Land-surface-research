Geographers divide the land into **natural regions**: areas where similar **climate, landforms, soils, vegetation and wildlife** occur together and shape similar ways of farming and living. The [Regions catalog](/wiki/regions/index.html) holds 99 of them, each linked to its [countries](/wiki/countries/index.html), [climate zones](/wiki/climates/index.html), [soils](/wiki/soils/index.html), [landforms](/wiki/landforms/index.html), [species](/wiki/species/index.html) and [crops](/wiki/crops/index.html).

## The chain of causes

:::collapse !Climate → water → soil → life → people
1. **Latitude and air circulation** set the basic climate: rainy tropics, dry subtropical deserts, stormy mid-latitudes, cold poles. See [Climate zones explained](/wiki/climate/climate-zones-explained.html).
2. **Mountains and oceans** modify it: the Western Ghats soak the Konkan and leave the Deccan dry; the cold Humboldt Current creates the Atacama.
3. **Rainfall and temperature** decide **weathering and leaching**, and with the **parent rock** they make the soil: black regur on Deccan basalt, red soils on granite, deep chernozem under steppe grass, leached laterite under monsoon rain, and frozen gelisols in the Arctic. See [What is soil?](/wiki/soil/what-is-soil.html).
4. **Climate and soil** decide the **vegetation**: rainforest, savanna, steppe, taiga, tundra, desert scrub.
5. **Vegetation** decides the **wildlife**: tigers in monsoon forests, saiga on the steppe, orangutans in Bornean rainforest.
6. Together they decide **what people can farm**: rice on wet alluvial deltas, wheat on cool dry plains, millets and pulses in semi-arid lands, tea and coffee on wet hill slopes, dates in oases.

:::

## Regions at a glance

| Region type | Typical climate | Typical soil | Signature crops | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alluvial river plains | Monsoon or humid subtropical | Alluvial soil | Rice, wheat, sugarcane | Indo-Gangetic Plain, Nile valley, Mekong |
| Basalt plateaus | Tropical savanna, semi-arid | Black cotton soil, vertisols | Cotton, soybean, sorghum | Deccan, Malwa |
| Monsoon hills | Tropical monsoon | Laterite, forest soils | Tea, coffee, spices, rubber | Western Ghats, Northeast India |
| Hot deserts | Hot desert | Aridisols | Dates, millets (irrigated wheat) | Thar, Sahara, Arabian Peninsula |
| Cold deserts | Cold desert | Aridisols, gelisols | Barley, apricots | Ladakh, Gobi, Tibetan Plateau |
| Temperate grasslands | Continental, semi-arid | Chernozem, mollisols | Wheat, maize, sunflower | Pontic steppe, Great Plains, Pampas |
| Rainforests | Tropical rainforest | Oxisols, ultisols | Cassava, cocoa, oil palm | Amazon, Congo, Borneo |
| Mediterranean lands | Mediterranean | Calcisols, terra rossa | Olives, grapes, citrus | Mediterranean Basin, California, Cape |
| Boreal and tundra | Subarctic, tundra | Podzols, gelisols | Little farming | Siberian taiga, Canadian Shield, Arctic |

## How to explore the links

Every region page lists its matching countries, climate zones, soils, landforms, signature species and crops. Every **species** page lists the countries and regions where it lives. Every **climate**, **soil** and **crop** page lists the regions and countries where it is found. A **country** page brings together its regions, climate zones, landforms, crops, fruits, protected areas, waterfalls and caves, and species.
