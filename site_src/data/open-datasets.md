---
title: Open Land Datasets
summary: Free datasets for maps of land cover, soils, climate, rainfall, forests, crops and drought, with what each is good for.
level: Reference
---
| Dataset | Provider | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/) | FAO | Crop and livestock production, land use, trade, by country since 1961 |
| [SoilGrids](https://soilgrids.org/) | ISRIC | Global 250 m maps of soil properties and classes |
| [Harmonized World Soil Database](https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/data-hub/soil-maps-and-databases/harmonized-world-soil-database-v20/en/) | FAO / IIASA | Global soil map |
| [Global Forest Watch](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/) | WRI / University of Maryland | Annual tree-cover loss since 2001 (Hansen et al.) |
| [TerraBrasilis (PRODES, DETER)](https://terrabrasilis.dpi.inpe.br/) | INPE, Brazil | Amazon and Cerrado deforestation |
| [Köppen-Geiger 1-km maps](https://www.gloh2o.org/koppen/) | Beck et al. | Present and future climate zones |
| [CHIRPS](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps) | UCSB Climate Hazards Center | Daily rainfall since 1981 |
| [IMD gridded rainfall](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/) | India Meteorological Department | 0.25° daily rainfall for India since 1901 |
| [ESA WorldCover](https://esa-worldcover.org/) | ESA | 10 m global land cover |
| [MODIS land products](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/) | NASA | Vegetation (NDVI), fires, land temperature |
| [GRACE / GRACE-FO](https://grace.jpl.nasa.gov/) | NASA / GFZ | Groundwater and water storage changes |
| [WGMS Fluctuations of Glaciers](https://wgms.ch/) | World Glacier Monitoring Service | Glacier mass balance |
| [Bhuvan](https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/) | ISRO NRSC | Indian land use, wasteland and soil maps |
| [Soil Health Card portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/) | Government of India | Farm-level soil tests |
| [Protected Planet (WDPA)](https://www.protectedplanet.net/) | UNEP-WCMC / IUCN | Every protected area on Earth |
| [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/) | Global Biodiversity Information Facility | 2+ billion species occurrence records |
| [IUCN Red List](https://www.iucnredlist.org/) | IUCN | Conservation status of species |
