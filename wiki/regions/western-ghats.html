🇮🇳 Western Ghats

Mountain range and rainforest

A UNESCO biodiversity hotspot older than the Himalaya, catching the monsoon for peninsular India.

TypeMountain range and rainforest
ContinentAsia
CountriesIndia
ClimateTropical monsoon (Am) on the west; rain shadow to the east
Rainfall (mm/yr)2,000–7,000 on windward slopes
TemperatureMild; cool on high plateaus

🧭 At a glance

Western Ghats.

Overview#

A UNESCO biodiversity hotspot older than the Himalaya, catching the monsoon for peninsular India.

Landscape#

Escarpment, sholas, plateaus, Nilgiri and Anamalai hills

Soils#

Laterite soil; Red soil; Forest and mountain soil

Vegetation#

Evergreen rainforest, shola-grassland, monsoon forest

Wildlife#

Lion-tailed macaque, Nilgiri tahr, Purple frog, Great hornbill, Malabar giant squirrel, Bengal tiger, Asian elephant

Crops & fruits#

Tea, Coffee (arabica), Black pepper, Cardamom, Rubber, Coconut

People & history#

Kodava, Toda, Kurumba and many forest communities

Threats & changes#

Mining, dams, plantations, landslides (Wayanad 2024)
