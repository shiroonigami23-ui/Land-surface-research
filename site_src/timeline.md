# Timeline

<div class="timeline">
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~4.4 bn yr ago</span>: Oldest known mineral grains (Jack Hills zircons) show early continental crust.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~470 Myr ago</span>: First land plants colonise the continents and start forming soils.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~335 Myr ago</span>: Supercontinent Pangaea assembles.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~200 Myr ago</span>: Pangaea begins to break apart.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~66 Myr ago</span>: Deccan Traps erupt; the dinosaurs go extinct.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~50 Myr ago</span>: India collides with Asia; the Himalaya begin to rise.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~2.6 Myr ago</span>: Ice Age cycles begin.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~20,000 yr ago</span>: Last Glacial Maximum; ice sheets cover much of North America and northern Europe.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~11,700 yr ago</span>: Holocene begins; climate stabilises.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~11,500 yr ago</span>: Farming begins in the Fertile Crescent.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~9,000 yr ago</span>: Rice in China, maize in Mexico, barley and zebu at Mehrgarh.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~5,000 yr ago</span>: The green Sahara dries out.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">~2200 BCE</span>: 4.2-kiloyear drought event.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1876–78</span>: Great Famine in India.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1930s</span>: Dust Bowl in the USA.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1965–66</span>: Indian droughts; Green Revolution begins.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">1994</span>: UN Convention to Combat Desertification adopted.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2015</span>: India's Soil Health Card scheme launched.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2023</span>: UN International Year of Millets; Canada's worst wildfire season.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">2025</span>: Glaciers lose 408 Gt of ice.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">Aug 2026</span>: UNCCD COP17 in Mongolia; Amazon deforestation at its lowest since 1988.</div>
<div class="ev"><span class="yr">Sep 2026</span>: India's monsoon ends at 87% of normal.</div>
</div>
